02ABOUT CREDENT

A hub built for Africa's next generation of innovators.

Credent Company Limited was created to be Africa's number one answer in the field of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics. It represents a hub for Africa's advanced science, technology, and innovation — driven through modernized and practical educational systems.

Credent operates through two flagship arms: the Credent Robotics Education Division, carrying schools from first principles to capstone, and the Credent Industrial Division, carrying the same intelligence onto the training floor.
