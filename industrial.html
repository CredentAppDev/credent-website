01WHAT WE DO

Four ways Credent works inside industry.

Everyone is handed the future and quietly left to figure it out alone. Not on our floor.

01

Workforce AI Training

Emrys guides each operator through a machine one small step at a time — asking them to try, checking they understood, and remembering every session. Training that never loses patience.

02

Robotics & Automation

From build design to deployment — Credent's robotics stack, with live 3D build animation, brings automation onto the line and shows your team exactly how it goes together.

03

Voice-Controlled Machines

Credent's own voice engine — detection, speech-to-text and speech, with Emrys as the brain. Say "lights on" and the floor responds. Hands stay on the work; the machine listens.

04

Readiness Analytics

Progress, impact and performance tracking across your workforce — so you know who is ready for which machine before the stakes are real.

02HOW A PILOT RUNS

From first visit to a trained floor.

Assess

We walk your floor with you — mapping the machines, the skills gaps, and where training breaks down today.

Load Emrys

Your manuals, SOPs and machine documentation go in — Emrys reads files, images and voice, and teaches from your operation, not a generic script.

Train & Verify

Operators learn step by step on the real machine, with Emrys checking understanding at every stage — and analytics showing you exactly who is ready.

Automate

Voice control and robotics integration follow — the floor starts to listen, and your trained team commands it.
