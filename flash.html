Flash your Mino

Plug your Mino (ESP32‑S3) into this computer with a USB‑C cable and click Flash Mino. No software to install — your browser does it. Works in Chrome and Edge on desktop.

Your browser can’t flash devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer (Web Serial isn’t available on Firefox, Safari, or most phones). This page must be served over https (or localhost) to access the USB serial port.
1
Connect Mino to this computer with a USB‑C data cable (not a charge‑only cable).
2
Click Flash Mino and pick the device’s serial port when prompted.
3
Wait for it to finish, then follow the on‑device setup: join the Credent‑Mino Wi‑Fi, enter your Wi‑Fi, and pair it here.
Building your own? Prefer to compile it yourself — grab the firmware source and flash with PlatformIO (pio run -t upload). The web flasher above is the no‑install path for everyone else.
