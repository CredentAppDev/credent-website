What Emrys actually does today

No over-promising. Every capability below is shipping in the beta — for the classroom and the training floor.

Emrys AI tutor

A patient teacher that teaches one small step at a time, remembers the session, checks understanding, and never just dumps answers.

Teach mode

Emrys delivers a full, ready-to-read lesson the teacher speaks to the class — even without prior coding experience.

Roadmaps & daily reports

Projects broken into day-by-day lessons. Teachers log what the class finished and Emrys plans the next step.

Understands files, images & voice

Send a PDF, a photo, code, or speak — and Emrys reads it and responds. This is the capability your launch is built around.

Group chat + voice/video calls

Classes and teams communicate in-app with group chat and live voice and video calls.

Remote control / screen share

A teacher or agent can guide a student's screen live, TeamViewer-style — always with the student's permission.

Memory across sessions

Emrys remembers what you were working on, lesson after lesson, so teaching picks up where it left off.

Analytics

Progress, impact, and class-performance tracking so you can see what's working.

Industrial training

The same agent that teaches a child to code trains an operator on the floor — step by step, before the stakes are real.

Not a chatbot bolted onto a school.

Credent is an agentic teaching system — software that teaches, remembers, and guides — built to carry both the classroom and the industrial training floor.
